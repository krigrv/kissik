## Open-source vs Google SDKs
- Default person/face engine: **MediaPipe Tasks 1.2.0** (Apache-2.0) with its Apache-licensed models bundled (`selfie_segmenter`, `blaze_face_short_range`). Version 1.2.0 is needed because 0.10.21's native library isn't 16 KB-aligned and won't load on Android 15+ 16 KB devices (checked on the emulator).
- ML Kit stays as a choice in Settings and as the automatic fallback. Barcodes, OCR and the document scanner still use ML Kit: Google's free SDKs, not open source. Possible open replacements: ZXing (Apache-2.0) for barcodes; Tesseract (Apache-2.0) for OCR at lower quality.
- whisper.cpp (MIT), RootEncoder (Apache-2.0), OkHttp (Apache-2.0), HaishinKit (BSD-3), kotlinx/AndroidX/Compose (Apache-2.0).
