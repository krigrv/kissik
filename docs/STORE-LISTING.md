**Description** (up to 10,000 characters):

Make any webcam, or your phone, look its best. KISSIK is a free, open-source camera studio for Windows.

Background
• Blur, replace with a picture or video, or remove the room behind you. Clean edges, on your own PC.
• Green screen, spotlight and retouch.

Look and framing
• Real camera controls: focus, zoom, exposure, white balance.
• Filters and your own LUTs.
• Auto framing keeps you in the picture; Smart framing moves in close.
• Up to 4K 60, vertical 9:16, and your own output profiles.
• Screen light turns your monitor into a soft key light.

Scenes
• Name cards, lower thirds, bubble cam, presentation and more.
• Your name, title, pronouns and logo fill every scene.

Meetings, recording and streaming
• KISSIK appears as a camera in Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Discord and OBS.
• Record exactly what you see, with sound. Recordings survive a crash.
• Replay buffer saves the last 30 seconds. Go live to YouTube and Twitch.

Your phone as a camera
• Install KISSIK Camera on Android, scan a QR code, and you're live.
• Up to 4K on back lenses, about 100 ms camera to screen at 60 fps.

Sound and access
• Microphone mixer, noise removal, EQ and limiter.
• Live captions, a prompter under your webcam, a privacy shutter.
• Simple mode: five big buttons. Works with keyboard and screen readers.

Private by design
Video, sound and AI all stay on your PC. No account and no tracking. Crash reports are off until you say yes.

Free and open source: github.com/krigrv/kissik

**Notes for certification:**
To test: connect any webcam and press Connect. To use KISSIK in a meeting app, open Meeting camera → Install the KISSIK camera (one Windows prompt), then pick "KISSIK" as the camera in Teams, Zoom or the Camera app. Phone camera: pick Phone (Wi-Fi) and scan the QR code with the KISSIK Camera Android app on the same Wi-Fi. No account or test credentials are needed.
